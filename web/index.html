SCIENTIFIC METHODOLOGY & VIVA PREPARATION

Project Architecture & 20 Viva Talking Points

1. Why use Fuzzy Logic for Cognitive Workload?

Unlike black-box deep learning, Fuzzy Mamdani inference provides 100% linguistic transparency (e.g., "IF Alpha power is LOW AND Theta/Alpha Ratio is HIGH THEN Workload is HIGH"), allowing clinicians and engineers to understand the exact physiological reasoning behind every classification.

2. Why use Subject-Wise Cross-Validation (StratifiedGroupKFold)?

Random train/test splits leak individual subject brain signatures, causing artificial over-optimism (>90% accuracy). Subject-wise CV ensures subjects in the test set were never seen during training, validating true generalization across people.

3. What is the physiological role of Theta & Alpha Ratios?

Increased mental workload increases frontal theta power (working memory demand) and suppresses parietal alpha power (cortical activation/desynchronization). Thus, the Theta/Alpha Ratio (TAR) is a well-established physiological indicator of cognitive load.

4. How does the system handle signal artifacts?

Raw EEG signals are bandpass-filtered (1–40 Hz), notch-filtered at 50 Hz (power line interference), re-referenced to average, and windowed with amplitude thresholding (±150 μV) to eliminate ocular and myogenic artifacts.
